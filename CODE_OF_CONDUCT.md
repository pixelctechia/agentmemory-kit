# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming space for anyone who wants to use,
discuss, or improve it — regardless of experience level, background, or
which AI coding tool they use.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct, but respectful. Disagreement about design decisions is fine;
  personal attacks are not.
- Assume good faith. Most confusing questions come from someone genuinely
  trying to understand, not from bad intent.
- Give context when reporting a problem: which tool (Claude Code,
  Antigravity, other), what you expected, what actually happened.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, or
  any other project space.
- Publishing anyone else's private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions —
and to any other setting where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the
maintainer directly. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues/PRs, or
ban contributors who violate this code of conduct, at their discretion.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is a short, plain-language adaptation of common
open-source community standards (e.g. the Contributor Covenant), kept
intentionally brief.
